Privacy and how Truth_Rail works

Short and plain. Please read it before you send anything personal.

What you send

Messages, links, screenshots, voice notes and PDFs that you send are read by AI services (Google Gemini and Groq) to find the claims and write the answer, and are searched against trusted websites. The free plans of AI providers may use inputs to improve their services, so do not send private or personal information such as bank details, OTPs, passwords, ID numbers or private photos.

What Truth_Rail keeps

  • Saved answers: short claim summaries taken from your message, the verdict and the source links, kept for a few hours (up to 24 hours) so repeated forwards are answered faster. Your original message text is not stored.
  • Usage log: the time, the type of input (text, image, voice, PDF, link), the verdict label, and a one-way scrambled ID instead of your phone number. It cannot be turned back into your number.
  • Voice replies: audio files are deleted automatically after about an hour.
  • Feedback: your thumbs up or down, with the verdict label, and nothing that identifies you.
  • On your device only: the website keeps your recent checks, language and text size in your own browser. "Clear" removes them.

What Truth_Rail does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no selling of data.
  • It only opens a link if the message is just that link, and it refuses private or internal addresses.

Please remember

  • Truth_Rail is a guide, not a final authority. It can be wrong. Always open the sources.
  • It is not medical, legal or financial advice. For health or money decisions, ask a qualified person.
  • When it says "Cannot be confirmed", that means no trusted source was found, not that the claim is true or false.

Truth_Rail is a student project built for the Hack On Track hackathon (GenAI, PS 3).
